Privacy Policy

Effective: July 14, 2026  |  Last updated: October 8, 2026

This is an English translation provided for your convenience. If there is any discrepancy between this translation and the Korean original, the Korean original prevails.

Chorditor (the "Company" or the "App") complies with applicable laws, including the Korean Personal Information Protection Act, and does its best to handle users' personal information safely. This Policy explains what personal information the Company collects and how it uses, stores, and shares it.

1. Personal Information Collected and How It Is Collected

CategoryItems collectedHow collected
Sign-up / sign-in Email address, social sign-in identifier, authentication token When you sign up or sign in through Supabase authentication
Optional information Gender, age range When you enter it on the onboarding screen after giving separate consent (not collected without consent)
Payment / subscription Subscription status, payment identifier (receipt token), purchase history When subscriptions are processed through Google Play in-app billing and RevenueCat
Service usage records Device identifier, app usage logs, screen view and event click records, error logs Collected automatically through Firebase Analytics (GA4)
Push notifications FCM push token, app language setting, device time zone Issued and stored automatically if you agree to receive push notifications (language and time zone are used to match the language and delivery time of notifications)
Advertising Advertising ID (ADID), device information, IP address, ad impression, click, and view records Collected automatically when rewarded ads are served through Google AdMob

2. Purposes of Collection and Use

  • Identifying members, keeping you signed in, and syncing data (Songs, chord charts) across devices
  • Processing payments for the Pro plan subscription and in-app items (Picks), and confirming purchase history
  • Analyzing service usage statistics, improving features, and diagnosing errors
  • Providing personalized content and information on your learning progress (if you consented to gender and age range)
  • Delivering notices, events, and push notifications (only if you consented)
  • Serving rewarded ads and measuring ad performance

3. Retention and Use Period

In principle, personal information is destroyed without delay when you delete your account. However, the following information is retained for the stated period for the reasons below.

  • Records of payment and supply of goods under the Korean Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce, etc.: 5 years
  • Records of consumer complaints or dispute resolution under the same Act: 3 years
  • Access records related to use of the Service under the Korean Protection of Communications Secrets Act: 3 months

4. Provision to Third Parties and Outsourcing of Processing

In principle, the Company does not provide users' personal information to outside parties. It outsources the processing of personal information for operating the Service as follows.

ProcessorOutsourced task
SupabaseMember authentication and database storage (Songs / projects)
Google FirebaseService usage analytics (Analytics), sending push notifications (FCM)
RevenueCat / Google PlayIn-app billing and subscription management

These processors may have servers overseas, in which case personal information may be transferred to and stored outside your country. In accordance with applicable law, the Company manages and supervises the processors through outsourcing agreements and other means so that they handle personal information safely.

The App provides rewarded ads (where in-app items, retries, and similar rewards are granted for watching an ad) through Google AdMob. AdMob collects and processes the advertising ID (ADID), device information, IP address, ad interaction records, and similar data on its own to serve ads and measure performance. This is not processing outsourced by the Company but Google's own independent processing of personal information. For details, please see Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Device Permissions

  • Storage access — Used to save the chord chart images you create to your device gallery. No other files are accessed.
  • Internet access — Used to provide the Service, including sign-in, data sync, font loading, and sending statistics.
  • Notification permission — Used to deliver notifications to users who agreed to receive push notifications.

6. Personalized Ads and Managing Your Advertising ID

You can opt out of personalized ads through your device settings. On Android devices, go to Settings > Google > Ads and choose "Opt out of Ads Personalization" or "Reset advertising ID." Non-personalized ads may still be shown.

7. Your Rights and How to Exercise Them

You may at any time request to view, correct, delete, or suspend processing of your personal information through the in-app settings or the account deletion feature. When you delete your account, the related personal information is destroyed without delay, except for the statutory retention periods in Section 3.

8. Destruction of Personal Information

When personal information is no longer needed, for example because the retention period has passed or the purpose of processing has been achieved, the Company destroys it without delay. Information in electronic file form is permanently deleted in a way that cannot be recovered. Information that must be kept by law is moved to a separate database or stored in a different location and managed safely.

9. Protection of Children's Personal Information

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14. If we become aware that personal information of a child under 14 has been collected, we destroy it without delay.
